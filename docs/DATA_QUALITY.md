The remaining records are `verification_status=unverified`, meaning transcribed-and-validated but not yet eyeballed against the source. Procedure and tooling in §8 below.

## How to verify a sample

Every record carries `source_url`, `retrieved_on`, `source_type` and `verification_status`.

```
python tools/verify_records.py --by "Your Name" -n 10 --open
python tools/verify_records.py --status
```

It samples unverified records, prints each one's fields beside its source URL, and writes back what you found — `human_verified`, or `disputed` with your note appended to `source_note`. `verified_on` and `verified_by` come from the run.

Checking by hand instead: open a record's `source_url` and compare the fields. `source_note` says how it was obtained — rows marked `tier=page_read` should match the linked document in detail, rows marked `tier=listing_only` are only guaranteed a correct title and URL, and their duration is deliberately empty. Then re-run the pipeline; the validator warns on any row marked verified without a date, so the trail can't be half-filled.
